Food and clothing are separate top-level categories. Men's, women's, children's and special/work clothing belong under clothing, not independent top-level categories.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested these two additions and delegated the detailed food subcategory choices.

**How to apply:** Preserve this grouping and existing listings when expanding the catalogue. Keep agriculture, industrial food production and wholesale groups available for their different purposes.
