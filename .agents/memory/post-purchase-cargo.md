---
name: Post-purchase cargo recommendations
description: Cargo suggestions after purchases are not automatic transport bookings.
---

After a successful buyer order, automatically offer cargo preparation. Keep it a suggestion, not an automatic load, driver assignment, offer acceptance, or payment.

**Why:** The user requested automatic cargo recommendations after buying. An order request is not a paid transaction; shipment dimensions, exact addresses, departure date, and a negotiated driver price still need confirmation.

**How to apply:** Cover both direct purchase and cart checkout, recommend only for confirmed server responses, and keep separate seller pickups separate. Prefill authorized product information and coarse regions only. Never infer kilograms or volume from sale quantity; require actual measurements before recommending a vehicle.
