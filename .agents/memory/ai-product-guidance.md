name: AI product guidance
description: Owner's intended customer-help scope for AI maslahat.
---

AI maslahat should inform customers what the app can do and how to use it,
not only advise on marketplace prices or selling.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested app-capability information for customers
inside AI maslahat.

**How to apply:** Keep product-use guidance in scope when changing this assistant.
Describe actual available features; distinguish future commercial terms from
live functionality and do not imply the AI performs customer actions.

The owner asked for a comprehensive in-app overview covering driver profiles,
ratings, countries, AI translation, listing creation, search, account functions,
safety and distribution.

**Why:** These topics describe both the intended product and features at
different stages of completion; repeating the specification as current
capabilities would mislead customers.

**How to apply:** Keep the overview inside AI maslahat and clearly separate
working screens from planned features. A backend endpoint without a customer
screen is not a completed user-facing feature. Public help should remain
available without spending the paid AI allowance.
