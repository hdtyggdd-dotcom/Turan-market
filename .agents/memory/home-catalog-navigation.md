name: Home and category navigation
description: Product requirement separating the home listing feed from category browsing.
---

Keep the home screen focused on products and ads. Browse the category tree through the dedicated “Kategoriyalar” navigation button, not a repeated category grid on home.

**Why:** On 2026-10-07 the owner explicitly asked for categories only under the category button, with products and ads on the home screen.

**How to apply:** Preserve this separation during future homepage and navigation changes. Do not reintroduce a catalog category grid above the home listing feed.
